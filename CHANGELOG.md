### Added

- The homepage, in English and German: ten projects in four groups, each with a screenshot, why it was made, what it does, its stack and where it runs, with more screenshots, details and links to fold out.
- A lightbox for the screenshots, with arrow keys and swiping.
- How I build: six habits that run through the projects.
- The technologies of all projects, to filter the projects by.
- A contact section, and a print layout that saves the page as a PDF with every project's details.
- Light and dark colors, following the system or chosen in the header.
- A page for addresses that do not exist.
- A compact dashboard, linked quietly from the footer: every project's deployment, repository, CI and coverage at a glance, with its Actions, issues, pull requests and downloads.
- My pronouns, she/her, in the greeting at the top of the page.
